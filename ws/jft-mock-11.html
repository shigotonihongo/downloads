こたえ · Answer Key

Each answer carries the reason it is correct — read the WHY even when you scored it right.

12たいせつ
This is a precious watch I got from my mother.
WHY 大切 is たいせつ (precious, important). 大 is たい here with no voiced だ, and 切 is せつ, not きり.
23ひがしぐち
I'm waiting at the station's east exit.
WHY 東口 is ひがしぐち (east exit). 東 takes its own reading ひがし, and 口 turns into ぐち after it.
31らいねん
I'm going back to my country next year.
WHY 来年 is らいねん (next year). 来 is らい in time words like 来週. くるとし is not a word, and きねん is a different word.
42電話
I'll give you a ___ later. Please tell me your number.
WHY You call a number with a 電話, a phone: 電話を かけます. 写真 is a photo and 新聞 is a newspaper.
53水
I was thirsty, so I drank some ___.
WHY When you are thirsty you drink 水, water. 火 is fire and 木 is a tree; you cannot drink them.
61道
Go straight along this ___ and you'll find the station.
WHY You go straight along a 道, a road. 空 is the sky and 耳 is an ear.
73おもい
This luggage is very ___, so I can't carry it alone.
WHY Luggage you cannot carry alone is おもい, heavy. かるい (light) is the opposite, and ひくい (low) is about height.
82つめたい
On hot days I want to drink ___ water.
WHY On a hot day you want つめたい, cold, water. ほそい (thin) and ひろい (wide) do not describe water.
91ひま
I have no work today, so I'm ___.
WHY No work means you are ひま, free. たいへん means hard or tough, and じょうず means good at something.
102毎朝まいあさ 歯はを みがきます。
Which sentence uses みがく correctly?
WHY Teeth are brushed with みがく: 歯を みがきます. A face and hair are あらう, washed.
111つよい 風かぜが ふいて います。
Which sentence uses ふく correctly?
WHY Wind ふく, blows: 風が ふいて います. Rain and snow ふる, fall; they never ふく.
123ぼうしを かぶって 出でかけました。
Which sentence uses かぶる correctly?
WHY A hat goes on with かぶる: ぼうしを かぶって. Shoes are はく and glasses are かける.
134に
A: Which drink would you like? B: Then I'll have coffee.
WHY Choosing is 〜に します: コーヒーに します, I'll have coffee. コーヒーを します would mean 'doing' coffee.
144食たべて いません
A: Have you had lunch yet? B: No, not yet.
WHY To もう 〜ましたか, 'not yet' is まだ 〜て いません: まだ 食べて いません. まだ 食べません sounds like you refuse to eat for now.
152ことが
A: Can you play the piano? B: Yes, I can play a little.
WHY Ability is the dictionary form + ことが できる: ひく ことが できます. ほうが, ものが and ときが do not join できる this way.
161と
A: What is the name of that flower? B: It's called sakura.
WHY A name is given with 〜と いいます: さくらと いいます. を, が and に cannot quote a name.
173でも
A: We're a bit tired, aren't we? B: Yes. How about some tea?
WHY でも makes a soft suggestion: お茶でも 飲みませんか, how about some tea? お茶しか would ask if tea is all they drink.
182く
A: How is it outside? B: The rain stopped and it got a little warmer.
WHY An い-adjective changes with 〜く なる: あたたかく なりました. に なる is for な-adjectives and nouns, like しずかに なる.
192おかえりなさい。
A: I'm home. B: ___
WHY ただいま, I'm home, is answered with おかえりなさい, welcome back. いってきます is said when you leave, おやすみなさい at bedtime.
201はい、かしこまりました。
A: Excuse me, two coffees, please. B: ___
WHY Staff take an order with かしこまりました, certainly. ごちそうさまでした is said after a meal and いただきます before one.
213いいえ、どういたしまして。
A: Thanks for helping me. B: ___
WHY Answer thanks with どういたしまして, you're welcome. はじめまして is for meeting someone new, and おやすみなさい is for bedtime.
222すみません、何時なんじまでですか。
A: ___ B: Until 9 p.m.
WHY 9時までです gives a closing time, so the question is 何時までですか, until what time. 何時から asks when it opens.
233失礼しつれいですが、お名前なまえは？
A: ___ B: I'm Yamada. Nice to meet you.
WHY 山田です answers a question about a name: 失礼ですが、お名前は？ お国は asks the country and お仕事は asks the job.
241すみません、写真しゃしんを とって くれませんか。
A: ___ B: Sure. Okay, say cheese.
WHY はい、チーズ is said when taking a photo, so A asked 写真を とって くれませんか. 見せて would mean 'show me a photo'.
