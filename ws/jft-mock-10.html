こたえ · Answer Key

Each answer carries the reason it is correct — read the WHY even when you scored it right.

11こんばん
Shall we have dinner together this evening?
WHY 今晩 is こんばん (this evening). 今 is read こん here, not いま, and 晩 is ばん with a plain ば.
23がいこく
My older brother works abroad.
WHY 外国 is がいこく (a foreign country). 外 is がい here, not そと, and 国 stays こく with no voiced ご.
32おとな
Adults are 500 yen each.
WHY 大人 is おとな (adult), a special reading for the whole word. だいじん is a different word, and おおひと is not a word.
42魚
I like ___ better than meat. I eat it every day.
WHY Something you compare with meat and eat every day is 魚, fish. 紙 is paper and 花 is a flower.
51雪
This morning a lot of ___ fell and the town turned white.
WHY What falls and turns a town white is 雪, snow. 風 is wind and 空 is the sky; neither ふる.
63足
I walked a lot yesterday, so my ___ hurt.
WHY After a lot of walking it is your 足, legs and feet, that hurt. 耳 is ear and 目 is eye.
71いそぎました
There was no time before the train, so I ___ to the station.
WHY No time before the train means you hurry: いそぎました. あそびました (played) and すわりました (sat down) do not fit 駅まで.
82あかるい
This room has big windows, so it is very ___.
WHY Big windows let the light in, so the room is あかるい, bright. くらい (dark) is the opposite, and windows do not make a room せまい (small).
93さがしました
I couldn't find my key, so I ___ my bag.
WHY When a key is missing you look for it: さがしました. うりました (sold) and おぼえました (memorised) make no sense with a bag.
101あたらしい くつを はいて、出でかけました。
Which sentence uses はく correctly?
WHY Shoes and trousers are worn with はく: くつを はいて. A hat is かぶる and a shirt is きる.
113雨あめが やんだので、外そとで あそびました。
Which sentence uses やむ correctly?
WHY Rain やむ, stops: 雨が やんだので. A train 止まる and a shop しまる — they never やむ.
122自転車じてんしゃに のって、学校がっこうへ 行いきます。
Which sentence uses のる correctly?
WHY You ride a bicycle with のる: 自転車に のって. Stairs are のぼる and a road is 歩く, never のる.
133と
A: What will you do in the summer holidays? B: I'm thinking of going back to my country.
WHY A plan you are thinking about is the volitional + と 思って います: 帰ろうと 思って います. を, に and で cannot quote a thought.
144までに
A: By when should I hand in the report? B: Please hand it in by Friday.
WHY A deadline for one action is までに: 金曜日までに 出して. まで is for something that goes on until then, like 金曜日まで 休みます.
151が
A: What do you want for your birthday? B: I want a new bicycle.
WHY ほしい is an adjective, so the thing you want takes が: 自転車が ほしいです. を is for verbs, as in 自転車を 買いたい.
164来こなくても いいです
A: Do I have to come at 7 tomorrow too? B: No, tomorrow is a day off, so ___.
WHY いいえ to 〜なければ なりませんか is 〜なくても いいです: you don't have to come. 来ても いいです only gives permission, which nobody asked for.
172ので
A: Why were you off yesterday? B: I had a fever, so I took the day off.
WHY A reason is ので: 熱が あったので、休みました. のに and けど mean 'although', which does not fit taking a day off for a fever.
181すぎて
A: Are you all right? B: I ate too much last night, and my stomach hurts.
WHY Too much is the ます-stem + すぎる: 食べすぎて, I ate too much. 食べやすくて means 'easy to eat', which would not hurt.
193すみません、これは いくらですか。
A: ___ B: That one is 300 yen.
WHY 300円 answers a question about price: これは いくらですか. 何ですか asks what it is, and だれのですか asks whose it is.
201いいですね。ぜひ。
A: Would you like to go to the mountains with me this Sunday? B: ___
WHY Accept an invitation warmly with いいですね。ぜひ — 'sounds great, I'd love to'. どういたしまして answers thanks, and おかげさまで answers 'how are you?'.
212あ、しつれいしました。
A: Excuse me, that's my seat. B: ___
WHY Sitting in someone's seat by mistake, apologise with しつれいしました. おじゃまします is for entering a home, and いただきます is before a meal.
223すみません、もう 少すこし ゆっくり お願ねがいします。
A: Press this button, then open here, then... B: ___
WHY When someone talks too fast, say もう 少し ゆっくり お願いします, a bit slower please. 大きい声で asks for louder, and 安く is for prices.
232うん、また あした。
A: Well, see you tomorrow. B: ___
WHY A friend's また あした is answered the same way: また あした, see you tomorrow. はじめまして is for a first meeting, and いただきます is before eating.
241あの 階段かいだんの 右みぎです。
A: Excuse me, where is the toilet? B: ___
WHY どこ asks where, so answer with a place: 階段の 右です, to the right of the stairs. The other two answer 何時 and いくら.
