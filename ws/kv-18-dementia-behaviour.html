1認知症にんちしょうninchishoudementia
2忘わすれるwasureruto forget — never test her memory

10安心あんしんanshinrelief; feeling safe
11見守みまもりmimamoriwatchful supervision — staying close, not locking doors
12そばsobabeside; close by — stay until she settles
