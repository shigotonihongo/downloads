こたえ · Answer Key

Each answer carries the reason it is correct — read the WHY even when you scored it right.

11せんしゅう
Last week I saw a film with a friend.
WHY 先週 is せんしゅう (last week). 先 is read せん here, not さき, and the long しゅう is needed: せんしゅ is a different word.
23いりぐち
Let's meet at the station entrance.
WHY 入口 is いりぐち (entrance): 口 becomes ぐち after いり. にゅう is how 入 reads in words like 入学, not here.
32まいあさ
I drink coffee every morning.
WHY 毎朝 is まいあさ (every morning), like まいにち. 朝 keeps its own reading あさ here; ちょう is used in words like 朝食.
43春
When ___ comes, the cherry blossoms come out.
WHY Cherry blossoms open in 春, spring. 秋 is autumn and 冬 is winter.
51薬
I have a fever, so I took the ___ I got at the hospital.
WHY What a hospital gives you for a fever is 薬, medicine. 茶 is tea and 酒 is alcohol.
62名前
"What is your ___?" "It's Kim."
WHY "Kim" answers a question about your 名前, your name. 仕事 is your job and 国 is your country.
73つかれました
I worked standing up all day, so I'm very ___.
WHY A long day on your feet leaves you つかれました, tired. わらいました (laughed) and はれました (cleared up) do not follow.
81おくりました
I ___ a letter to my mother back home.
WHY A letter to someone far away is sent: 手紙を おくりました. ひろいました (picked up) and すてました (threw away) make no sense with 母に.
92やさしかった
This test was ___, so I finished quickly.
WHY Finishing quickly means the test was やさしかった, easy. むずかしかった (difficult) is the opposite, and おもい (heavy) is for things you lift.
102かぜを ひいて、仕事しごとを 休やすみました。
Which sentence uses ひく correctly?
WHY You catch a cold with ひく: かぜを ひきました. A shower is あびる and a hat is かぶる.
113けがが なおって、サッカーを しました。
Which sentence uses なおる correctly?
WHY An injury or illness なおる, gets better: けがが なおって. Rain やむ (stops), and a ticket does not get better.
122あさ シャワーを あびます。
Which sentence uses あびる correctly?
WHY You あびる a shower: シャワーを あびます. Coffee is のむ and a newspaper is よむ.
133あとで
A: ___ work is over, shall we go and eat together? B: Sounds good. Let's go.
WHY After an action is the た-form + あとで: 終わった あとで. まえに needs the dictionary form (終わる まえに), and ながら joins a ます-stem.
142かもしれません
A: Is there football practice tomorrow? B: I don't know yet. It ___ rain.
WHY Not knowing yet calls for a guess: ふる かもしれません, it might rain. ことに します is a decision, and nobody decides the rain.
154に
A: How old is your younger brother? B: He turns twenty next month.
WHY Becoming something is noun + に なります: 二十歳に なります. を, で and が cannot mark what you become.
161ほう
A: Which is faster, the train or the bus? B: The train is faster.
WHY Answering どちら, the winner takes の ほうが: 電車の ほうが 速いです. より marks the loser (バスより), not the winner.
171ねても
A: How are you feeling? B: I slept a lot, and I still have a fever.
WHY A result against what you expect takes 〜ても: ねても, even though I slept. ねたら and ねるので expect the fever to go, which まだ rules out.
182だけ 買かいました
A: How many apples did you buy? B: They were expensive, so I bought ___ one.
WHY Only one, with a positive verb, is だけ: 一つ だけ 買いました. しか needs a negative verb (一つしか 買いませんでした).
193キムです。どうぞ よろしく。
A: Nice to meet you. I'm Yamada. B: ___
WHY A first meeting ends with どうぞ よろしく. おひさしぶりです is for people you have not seen for a while, and どういたしまして answers thanks.
201いってらっしゃい。
A: I'm off now. B: ___
WHY いってきます is answered with いってらっしゃい. おかえりなさい is for someone coming home, and おやすみなさい is for bedtime.
212はい、お願ねがいします。
A: Do you need a carrier bag? B: ___
WHY Accepting something a clerk offers is はい、お願いします. どういたしまして answers thanks, and おまたせしました is what the clerk says.
222いろいろ お世話せわに なりました。
A: So you go back to your country next month. We'll miss you. B: ___
WHY Leaving a place where people helped you, say お世話に なりました. ごちそうさまでした is only for food.
231もしもし、キムです。
A: ___ B: Oh, Kim? It's Tanaka.
WHY Answering the phone, say もしもし. Tanaka already knows Kim, so はじめまして does not fit, and いってきます is for leaving home.
243それは ざんねんですね。
A: Tomorrow's trip is off because of the rain. B: ___
WHY Bad news like a cancelled trip gets ざんねんですね. よかったですね and おめでとうございます are for good news.
