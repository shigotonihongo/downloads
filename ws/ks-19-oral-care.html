口腔こうくうケア：食事しょくじの あとで

Oral Care — Rinsing, Brushing, and Dentures After a Meal — Scenario Dialogue

Can-do: Offer oral care after a meal: seat the resident upright, rinse first, brush gently, take dentures away to clean, and watch for coughing the whole time.

1 · Dialogue & Why — 会話

Hana (caregiver)
田中たなかさん、口腔こうくうケアを しましょうか。
Tanaka-san, shall we do your mouth care?
Why: 〜しましょうか invites rather than announces. Oral care goes INSIDE the mouth, so ask before you start every time — consent matters most where the care is most intimate.
Tanaka-san (resident)
ええ、お願ねがいします。
Yes, please do.
Why: A short acceptance. Wait for it. If it does not come, you do not start — the same rule as Ep 14.
Hana (caregiver)
いすに 座すわったままで 大丈夫だいじょうぶですよ。
Staying sitting up in the chair is fine.
Why: THE SAFETY POINT OF THIS EPISODE. Never lay a resident flat for oral care — water runs into the airway. Upright, chin slightly down. 〜たままで means 'staying as you are'.
Hana (caregiver)
まず、うがいを しましょう。お水みずを どうぞ。
First let's rinse. Here's some water.
Why: Rinse BEFORE brushing: it loosens what is there and tells you how well they can hold and spit water. まず orders the steps out loud, which is what makes care predictable.
Tanaka-san (resident)
はい。……あ、すっきりしました。
Alright. Ah, that feels better.
Why: すっきり is the word residents actually use after care that leaves them clean — you met it in Ep 3 and Ep 4. Hearing it is your confirmation that the step went well.
Hana (caregiver)
つぎは 歯はを みがきますね。ゆっくりで 大丈夫だいじょうぶですよ。
Next I'll brush your teeth. There's no rush.
Why: Say what comes next before your hand moves. ゆっくりで 大丈夫[だいじょうぶ]ですよ (Ep 1) does as much work here as it does at waking — a mouth being cleaned by someone else is a tense thing.
Tanaka-san (resident)
入いれ歯ばも ありますよ。
I have dentures too.
Why: 入[い]れ歯[ば] is the everyday word a resident uses; 義歯[ぎし] is the one on the care record. Learn to recognise both and to SAY the friendly one.
Hana (caregiver)
はい、あとで きれいに 洗あらいますね。
Yes, I'll wash them clean afterwards.
Why: Dentures come out and are cleaned separately, every day. Say what you are doing with them — a resident whose teeth leave the room wants to know they are coming back.
Hana (caregiver)
むせないように 気きをつけてくださいね。
Please be careful not to choke.
Why: The same sentence as Ep 2 at mealtimes, because the same risk is present. If a cough starts, STOP, sit them further forward, and report it — a cough during oral care is how 誤嚥[ごえん] shows itself.
Hana (caregiver)
終おわりました。お口くちの 中なかを 確認かくにんしますね。
All done. I'll just check inside your mouth.
Why: Always look afterwards: food left in a cheek is what causes trouble overnight. 確認[かくにん]します (Ep 18) is the same verb you use before a handover, and for the same reason.
