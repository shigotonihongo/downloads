Caregiver Vocabulary: Symptoms & Condition

Can-do: say and understand 12 everyday caregiver symptoms condition fever pain itching constipation diarrhoea swelling appetite cough sluggish how a resident is today report to the nurse words.

1 · Vocabulary — 語彙

#日本語RomajiEnglishメモ / Notes
1体調たいちょうtaichouphysical condition; how a resident is in themselves today
2調子ちょうしchoushicondition; how things are going (調子[ちょうし]は いかがですか = how are you feeling?)
3発熱はつねつhatsunetsufever; a temperature above the resident's own baseline
4痛いたみitamipain (痛[いた]みは ありますか = is there any pain?)
5かゆみkayumiitching; itchiness — check the skin, never scratch it for them
6便秘べんぴbenpiconstipation
7下痢げりgeridiarrhoea; loose stool
8むくみmukumiswelling; oedema — often the feet and ankles by evening
9食欲しょくよくshokuyokuappetite (食欲[しょくよく]が ない = they have no appetite)
10咳せきsekia cough
11だるいdaruisluggish; heavy and tired all over
12様子ようすyousuhow someone is; the state you observe and pass on
