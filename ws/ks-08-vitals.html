バイタル 測定そくてい：体温たいおんと 血圧けつあつ

Taking Vital Signs — Scenario Dialogue

Can-do: Explain and take a resident's temperature and blood pressure, and reassure them about the results.

1 · Dialogue & Why — 会話

Hana (caregiver)
山田やまださん、体温たいおんを 測はかりますね。
Yamada-san, I'll take your temperature.
Why: 測はかる = 'to measure' — the one verb for taking temperature, blood pressure, pulse, weight. 〜ますね softly tells the resident what you're about to do before you do it — always narrate your actions in care so nothing surprises them.
Yamada-san (resident)
はい、お願ねがいします。
Yes, please go ahead.
Why: お願ねがいします here means 'please (do it for me)' — agreeing to and inviting the action. Short, natural resident response.
Hana (caregiver)
わきの下したに 入いれますね。 じっと していてください。
I'll put it under your arm. Please stay still.
Why: わきの下した = 'armpit / under the arm' (わき + の + 下した). じっとしていてください = 'please stay still' — じっと is 'motionless'. Clear, gentle instructions keep the reading accurate and the resident comfortable.
Hana (caregiver)
36度ど8分ぶです。 平熱へいねつですね。
It's 36.8 degrees. That's a normal temperature.
Why: Temperature decimals are read with 分ぶ (ぶ): 36.8 → 36度ど8分ぶ. 平熱へいねつ = 'normal (healthy) temperature'. Say the number AND what it means — residents relax when you interpret the result for them.
Hana (caregiver)
次つぎに 血圧けつあつを 測はかります。 少すこし 腕うでを しめますよ。
Next I'll take your blood pressure. It'll squeeze your arm a little.
Why: 次つぎに = 'next'. 血圧けつあつ = 'blood pressure'. しめる = 'to tighten/squeeze' — warning the resident that the cuff will press (少すこし = 'a little') prevents them from being startled. The よ gently alerts them.
Yamada-san (resident)
よろしく お願ねがいします。
Please, go ahead.
Why: よろしくお願ねがいします = a soft 'I leave it to you / please take care of it'. Very common when handing yourself over to someone's care or service.
Hana (caregiver)
上うえが 130、下したが 80です。 いい 数字すうじですよ。
130 over 80. Those are good numbers.
Why: For blood pressure, 上うえ ('upper') = systolic and 下した ('lower') = diastolic — so 上うえが130、下したが80 = '130 over 80'. 数字すうじ = 'numbers/figures'. Reassuring with 'いい数字すうじですよ' turns a reading into comfort.
Yamada-san (resident)
安心あんしんしました。
I'm relieved.
Why: 安心あんしんする = 'to feel relieved / at ease'. 安心あんしんしました (past) = 'I felt relieved' — a natural, warm response to good news. A word you'll want residents to feel often.
Hana (caregiver)
お疲つかれさまでした。 ゆっくり 休やすんでくださいね。
Thank you for your patience. Please rest and take it easy.
Why: お疲つかれさまでした = 'thank you for your effort' — said after any shared task (a check-up, work, an activity). 休やすんでください = 'please rest'. Close every care interaction warmly.
