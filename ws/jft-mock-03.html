こたえ · Answer Key

Each answer carries the reason it is correct — read the WHY even when you scored it right.

1A洗あらいます
I get up in the morning and ___ my face.
WHY 顔かお goes with 洗あらう (to wash). The other three verbs never take 顔かお as their object.
2A重おもい
This luggage is ___. Let's carry it together.
WHY 重おもい = heavy — the reason two people carry it. 安やすい is price, 暖あたたかい is temperature, 楽たのしい is feeling.
3B郵便局ゆうびんきょく
A letter and stamps. I'm going to the ___.
WHY 切手きって (stamps) are sold at the 郵便局ゆうびんきょく. The others are library, park and hospital.
4D測はかります
I ___ the body temperature.
WHY 体温たいおん・血圧けつあつ・脈みゃく all take 測はかる. Saying 体温たいおんを します is a common beginner slip.
5C脱水だっすい
If you don't drink much water, you get ___.
WHY 脱水だっすい = dehydration, the risk when 水分すいぶん is low. This is why 水分すいぶん is recorded on every shift.
6A電車でんしゃ
I go to the station by ___.
WHY で after a noun of transport = by that means. 名札なふだ・制服せいふく・荷物にもつ are things you wear or carry.
7B疲つかれました
Work was busy today. I'm very ___.
WHY 忙いそがしい → 疲つかれる (to get tired). 助たすかる is what you say when someone HELPS you, not after a hard day.
8B転ころびました
A resident ___. I'll call the nurse right away.
WHY 転倒てんとう (a fall) is the one event that always means calling the 看護師かんごし immediately. The other three need no nurse.
9A顔色かおいろ
Your ___ looks bad. Please rest a little.
WHY 顔色かおいろが 悪わるい = looking pale or unwell — a fixed phrase, and the first thing a caregiver reports.
10C寝ね
I didn't ___ well yesterday. I'm sleepy today.
WHY 寝ねる = to sleep; 眠ねむい = sleepy is the result. 寝ねる is the action, 眠ねむい is the feeling.
11C開あけましょう
The room is hot. Let's ___ the window.
WHY 窓まどを 開あける = to open a window. 着きる is for clothes, 押おす for buttons, 呼よぶ for people.
12B温度おんど
The bath water's ___ is 40 degrees.
WHY 度ど of お湯ゆ is 温度おんど (temperature). 体温たいおん is body heat; 温度おんど is everything else.
13Dどうぞ 召めし上あがって ください。
(You bring the resident's meal) You: ___
WHY 召めし上あがる is the 尊敬語そんけいご of 食たべる, used FOR the resident. いただきます is 謙譲語けんじょうご: you say it about your own eating.
14Cはい、承知しょうちしました。
(Senior: Please handle this report.) You: ___
WHY 承知しょうちしました is the polite understood to a superior. 無理むりです refuses, and the other two belong to different situations.
15Dすみません、薬くすりを 落おとしました。すぐに 報告ほうこくします。
(You have dropped a resident's medicine) You: ___
WHY 誤薬ごやく is the accident this prevents: a dropped tablet is reported at once, never judged 大丈夫だいじょうぶ by yourself. 報連相ほうれんそう protects you as much as the resident.
16Bはい、ご案内あんないします。
(Resident: I want to go to the toilet.) Politely: ___
WHY 排泄はいせつ never waits — 我慢がまん costs 尊厳そんげん. ご案内あんないします is the polite I will take you there.
17Bゆっくり 立たちましょうか。
(A resident is standing up) Politely: ___
WHY 〜ましょうか invites, and moves together with the person. 立たて and 立たつな are commands — never used to a 利用者りようしゃ.
18D田中たなかさん、おはようございます。
(Addressing a resident) Which is correct?
WHY Always surname ＋ さん. あなた is cold to a resident, and a bare surname or おい is rude — the most common mistake of all.
19Dどこが 痛いたいですか。すぐに 看護師かんごしを 呼よびます。
(Resident: It hurts here.) Politely: ___
WHY 痛いたみの 訴うったえ is always taken seriously: ask WHERE, then report. Telling someone to 我慢がまん, or deciding 大丈夫だいじょうぶ yourself, is not the caregiver's call.
20Cこんにちは。お待まちして おりました。
(A resident's family arrives) You: ___
WHY お待まちして おります is 謙譲語けんじょうご — humble, warm, and the standard greeting to 家族かぞく. The others are used inside your own home.
21Aお疲つかれさまでした。
(To a senior leaving at the end of their shift) You: ___
WHY お疲つかれさまでした is the standard end-of-shift phrase in every Japanese workplace, and it is safe in every direction.
22Cそうですか。後あとで もう 一度いちど 聞ききますね。
(Resident: I do not want a bath.) Politely: ___
WHY 拒否きょひ is answered by accepting it now and asking again later. 無理強むりじい breaks 信頼関係しんらいかんけい and makes the next refusal harder.
23D10時じ30分ぷん
What time does the exercise session start?
A 今日きょうの 体操たいそうは 10時じからですね。
B いいえ、30分ぷん 遅おそく なりました。
A はい、分わかりました。
WHY It was 10時じ, but B says 30分ぷん 遅おそく なりました — so it starts at 10時じ30分ぷん.
24Aお風呂ふろ
Where is Tanaka-san now?
A 田中たなかさんは 部屋へやに いますか。
B いいえ、部屋へやには いません。今いま お風呂ふろです。
WHY B says 部屋へやには いません, then 今いま お風呂ふろです — the trap is answering from the first noun you hear.
25B37度ど5分ぶ
What was Sato-san's temperature this morning?
A 佐藤さとうさんの 平熱へいねつは 36度ど5分ぶです。
B 今朝けさは 37度ど5分ぶでした。少すこし 高たかいです。
WHY 36度ど5分ぶ is the 平熱へいねつ (baseline); 今朝けさ it was 37度ど5分ぶ. The 平熱へいねつ is never the answer.
26Aお茶ちゃ
What will the resident drink?
A お水みずは いかがですか。
B いいえ、お茶ちゃが いいです。
WHY 水みず is offered and refused; お茶ちゃ is what is wanted. Both are drinks, so the answer is in the second line.
27C看護師かんごしを 呼よびます
What will B do next?
A 田中たなかさんが 頭あたまが 痛いたいと 言いって います。
B 分わかりました。すぐに 看護師かんごしを 呼よびます。
WHY 頭あたまが 痛いたい → the immediate action is 看護師かんごしを 呼よぶ. 家族かぞくへの 連絡れんらく comes later and B never says it.
28D600円えん
How much is it in total?
A 弁当べんとうは 500円えんです。
B じゃあ、弁当べんとうと 100円えんの お茶ちゃを ください。
WHY 弁当べんとう 500円えん ＋ お茶ちゃ 100円えん ＝ 600円えん. JFT listening almost always needs one small sum.
29D手洗てあらい
What will B do first?
A 体操たいそうが 終おわりました。もうすぐ 食事しょくじです。
B はい、まず 手洗てあらいを して もらいます。
WHY 食事しょくじ is coming, so 手洗てあらい comes first — まず marks the order. 体操たいそう is mentioned as finished.
30C杖つえ
What will B bring?
A 田中たなかさんが 歩あるきます。薬くすりは もう 飲のみました。
B はい、杖つえを 持もって きますね。
WHY 田中たなかさん is about to 歩あるく, so B brings the 杖つえ. 薬くすり is mentioned as already taken — a classic distractor.
31C10時じ
What time does the exercise session start?
WHY The notice says 10時じから. 3時じ is when 歌うた happens, not 体操たいそう.
32Aお茶ちゃを 飲のんで もらう
What should you do for Tanaka-san?
WHY 熱ねつ means no bath today, and 水分すいぶん instead. The memo says exactly two things; everything else is invented.
33Aいいえ、店みせは 休やすみです
Can you shop on the weekend?
WHY 週末しゅうまつは 休やすみです — the opening hours in line 1 are weekdays only. Read to the last line before answering.
34D2かい
How many times a day is the medicine taken?
WHY 朝あさと 夜よる = twice. 昼ひるは 飲のみません is there precisely to catch the three-times-a-day guess.
35B3かい
How many times did Sato-san go to the toilet last night?
WHY 夜勤やきんの 報告ほうこく says 3かい. The handover exists so the day shift knows a night it did not see.
36D電車でんしゃ
Which is faster to the hospital?
WHY 電車でんしゃ 20分ぷん against バス 40分ぷん → 電車でんしゃ. The question asks which is FASTER, not which is cheaper.
37B来月らいげつ
When is the daughter coming?
WHY 来月らいげつ 行いきます. 週末しゅうまつ appears in the letter, but it belongs to the 電話でんわ, not the visit.
38A名札なふだ
What does this person put on?
WHY 名札なふだを つけて ください is the instruction; 制服せいふく is where it is kept, not what you attach.
39B高たかいです
Compared with the baseline, how is this morning's temperature?
WHY 36度ど5分ぶ → 37度ど8分ぶ is higher. Note 高たかい for temperature; 安やすい is only ever price.
40D先輩せんぱい
Who should this person consult?
WHY 分わからない ことは 先輩せんぱいに — 報連相ほうれんそう in one line. Guessing alone is the behaviour this rule exists to stop.
