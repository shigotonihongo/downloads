こたえ · Answer Key

Each answer carries the reason it is correct — read the WHY even when you scored it right.

1D店みせ
You want to buy a bag. Where do you go?
WHY 買かう (to buy) happens at a 店みせ (shop). 駅えき=station, 病院びょういん=hospital, 公園こうえん=park — none of them sell things.
2A週末しゅうまつ
Saturday and Sunday together are called the ___.
WHY 週しゅう=week + 末まつ=end → 週末しゅうまつ is the weekend. 毎日まいにち=every day, 去年きょねん=last year, 来月らいげつ=next month.
3D読よみます
Every morning I ___ the newspaper.
WHY A 新聞しんぶん (newspaper) is something you 読よむ (read). 飲のむ=drink, 着きる=wear, 聞きく=listen.
4B足あし
You use your ___ when you walk.
WHY 歩あるく (to walk) uses 足あし (legs/feet). 手て=hands, 歯は=teeth, 耳みみ=ears.
5C田中たなかさんは 会社かいしゃへ 行いきませんでした。
'Tanaka-san took the day off work.' Which sentence is closest in meaning?
WHY 会社かいしゃを 休やすむ = not go to work at all. Leaving early, working, and being late all mean the person DID go in.
6C三時半さんじはん
It's 3:00 now. Thirty minutes later it will be ___.
WHY 半はん = half past, so 三時さんじ + 30分ぷん = 三時半さんじはん. The other three are whole hours far from now.
7B上うえ
I have a younger brother and a younger sister. Of the three of us I am the ___ one.
WHY 弟おとうと and 妹いもうと are both YOUNGER, so you are 一番いちばん 上うえ (the eldest). 下した=below/youngest; 前まえ/後うしろ describe position in space, not age.
8A朝あさごはん
The meal you eat after getting up in the morning is ___.
WHY 朝あさ (morning) + ごはん (meal) = breakfast. 弁当べんとう=packed lunch, 果物くだもの=fruit, 野菜やさい=vegetables are foods, not meal times.
9C測はかります
I ___ the resident's temperature.
WHY 体温たいおん (body temperature) goes with 測はかる (to measure). 洗あらう=wash, 開あける=open, 押おす=push.
10A杖つえ
The resident cannot walk alone. They use a ___.
WHY 杖つえ is a walking stick — the support for someone who cannot walk unaided. 薬くすり=medicine, 新聞しんぶん=newspaper, 名札なふだ=name badge.
11B安やすい
This book is 1,000 yen. That book is 300 yen. That book is ___.
WHY 三百さんびゃく円えん < 千せん円えん, so it is 安やすい (cheap). 高たかい is the opposite; 古ふるい=old and 重おもい=heavy say nothing about price.
12C田中たなかさんは 病気びょうきです。
'Tanaka-san is not well.' Which sentence is closest in meaning?
WHY 元気げんきでは ありません = not well → 病気びょうきです (is ill). 忙いそがしい=busy and 楽たのしい=having fun are not about health, and なりました says the opposite.
13D木曜日もくようび
Today is Wednesday. Tomorrow is ___.
WHY The week runs 月げつ・火か・水すい・木もく・金きん・土ど・日にち, so the day after 水曜日すいようび is 木曜日もくようび. 火曜日かようび is the day BEFORE.
14D三百さんびゃく円えん
I bought three 100-yen bread rolls. In total that is ___.
WHY 百円ひゃくえん × 三みっつ = 三百さんびゃく円えん. ぜんぶで = in total.
15A働はたらきます
Every day I ___ at the company from morning until night.
WHY 会社かいしゃで 朝あさから 夜よるまで = all day AT the company → 働はたらく (to work). 休やすむ=rest, 寝ねる=sleep, 帰かえる=go home.
16Aいってきます。
(Leaving the house in the morning) You say: ___
WHY いってきます is said by the person LEAVING. ただいま=I'm home, おかえりなさい=welcome back (said TO them), おやすみなさい=good night.
17Bに
I'm meeting a friend ___ Sunday.
WHY に marks a POINT IN TIME with a specific day or clock time: 日曜日にちようびに, 三時さんじに. で marks a place of action, を an object, へ a direction.
18Dを
Go straight ___ this road.
WHY を marks the path you move ALONG or THROUGH: 道みちを 行いく, 公園こうえんを 歩あるく. に/へ mark a destination, で a place where an action happens.
19Aから
I eat after washing my hands.
WHY て-form + から = after doing. The action before から happens FIRST — wash, THEN eat. まで=until, ので=because, けど=but.
20Dたい
I want to go to Japan.
WHY ます-stem + たい = want to do. 行いきます → 行いきたいです. ながら=while doing, ましょう=let's, ください=please (a request, not a wish).
21Cないで
Please do not run in the hallway.
WHY ない-form + で + ください = please DON'T. 走はしる → 走はしらないで ください. なくて/ないと/なければ all start conditions or reasons, not a request.
22Bながら
I study while listening to music.
WHY ます-stem + ながら = do two things at once. The ながら action is the BACKGROUND one (listening); the main verb is 勉強べんきょうします.
23C一番いちばん
Of all fruit, I like apples the ___.
WHY 〜の 中なかで … 一番いちばん = the most, out of a group. とても=very and 少すこし=a little do not compare; あまり needs a negative verb.
24Bあまり
I don't read Japanese newspapers much.
WHY あまり pairs with a NEGATIVE: あまり 読よみません = don't read much. とても/たくさん/よく all take a positive verb.
25C読よめ
I can read a little kanji.
WHY Potential form: Group 1 verbs shift the う-row to the え-row + る — 読よむ → 読よめる → 読よめます (can read). 読よみます just means 'I read'.
26Dあれば
If I have time, I'll go.
WHY ば-form states the CONDITION: ある → あれば = if there is. あるから=because there is, あっても=even if there is, あって is just the て-form.
27Aと
When spring comes, the cherry blossoms bloom.
WHY plain form + と = a natural, always-true result: whenever A happens, B happens. It cannot be followed by a request or a command.
28C何なん
'How old are you?' 'I'm 25.'
WHY 何なん + counter asks HOW MANY: 何なん歳さい=how old, 何なん時じ=what time. どこ=where, だれ=who, いつ=when — none of them take 歳さい.
29Cそう
That person looks very busy.
WHY i-adjectives DROP the final い before そう: 忙いそがしい → 忙いそがしそう = looks busy (what you judge from seeing them).
30C話はなせる ように
Put the four parts in order. Which one goes in ★?
WHY Correct order: 日本語にほんごを 上手じょうずに 話はなせる ように なりたい からです。〜ように なる takes the POTENTIAL form (話はなせる), and なりたい must sit next to からです — so the third blank ★ is 話はなせる ように.
31B午前ごぜん 10時じ
What time does the meeting start?
WHY 午前ごぜん 10時じから 11時じまで — から marks the start, まで the end. 午後ごご would be the afternoon.
32A車くるま
How is Aya-san going to the office?
WHY 大雨おおあめです。ですから、車くるまで 行いきます — ですから introduces the result, and で marks the means of transport.
33D平熱へいねつです。
How is Tanaka-san's temperature now?
WHY 今朝けさ 熱ねつが ありました (past) but 今いまは 平熱へいねつです — 平熱へいねつ is a normal temperature, so the fever is gone.
34B火曜日かようび
When can you NOT go to the library?
WHY 火曜日かようびは 休やすみです — 休やすみ here means closed. The other three days are open, just with different hours.
35D友ともだちと 歌うたを 歌うたいます。
What does this person do on Saturdays?
WHY 毎週まいしゅう 土曜日どようびに 友ともだちと 歌うたを 歌うたいます. 家いえで 休やすむのは 日曜日にちようび — that is the OTHER day.
36B朝あさと 夜よる、食事しょくじの 後あと
When should the medicine be taken?
WHY 食事しょくじの 後あとに 飲のんで ください + 朝あさと 夜よる、一日いちにち 二に回かい — after meals, twice a day, morning and night.
37A午前ごぜん 7時じ
Until what time does the new night shift run?
WHY 午後ごご 10時じから 午前ごぜん 7時じまで — the shift crosses midnight, so it ENDS in the 午前ごぜん (a.m.).
38B先輩せんぱいに 伝つたえます。
What should Yamada-san do?
WHY 「先輩せんぱいに 伝つたえて ください」is the request. The hospital is 田中たなかさん's own day.
39A9時じ
From what time does this person work?
WHY 9時じから 働はたらきます. 6時じ=get up, 7時じ=breakfast, 8時じ=leave the house.
40D来年らいねん
When is this person going back to their home country?
WHY 来年らいねん 国くにへ 帰かえります — 来年らいねん is next year. 去年きょねん is last year, which is already past.
