こたえ · Answer Key

Each answer carries the reason it is correct — read the WHY even when you scored it right.

12らいげつ
I'm going back to my country next month.
WHY 来月 is らいげつ (next month), like 先月 and 今月. がつ is for month names such as 一月.
21すいようび
Wednesday is my day off work.
WHY 水曜日 is すいようび (Wednesday). 水 alone is みず, but in the day name it is read すい.
33ふるい
This bicycle is already old.
WHY 古い is ふるい (old). ふとい is 太い (thick) and ふかい is 深い (deep) — similar sounds, different kanji.
42雨
___ is falling, so I'll take an umbrella.
WHY Something that ふる (falls) and needs an umbrella is 雨, rain. 空 is the sky and 風 is wind — neither falls.
53先生
At school I'm learning Japanese from a ___.
WHY At school you learn from a 先生, a teacher. 学生 is a student, and a 店員 works in a shop.
61時計
When I looked at the ___, it was already nine.
WHY You look at a 時計, a clock, to see the time. 写真 is a photo and 地図 is a map.
72ねむい
I stayed up late yesterday, so today I'm very ___.
WHY Staying up late makes you ねむい, sleepy. あまい (sweet) and ひろい (wide) do not follow from a late night.
83しずか
Please be ___ in the library.
WHY In a library you keep しずか, quiet. げんき (lively, well) and べんり (convenient) are not what a library asks for.
91わすれました
I ___ my homework at home. I apologised to the teacher.
WHY You apologise because you left it behind: 家に わすれました. おぼえました (memorised) and おしえました (taught) need no apology.
102友達ともだちに 電話でんわを かけます。
Which sentence uses かける correctly?
WHY 電話を かける is 'to make a phone call'. You give (あげる) a present and send (送る) an email - you do not かける them.
112げんかんで くつを ぬぎます。
Which sentence uses ぬぐ (to take off) correctly?
WHY Shoes and clothes are ぬぐ: くつを ぬぎます. Glasses and a watch are はずす (めがねを はずします).
121駅えきの 前まえは 人ひとが 多おおくて にぎやかです。
Which sentence uses にぎやか correctly?
WHY にぎやか describes a place full of people and noise: 駅の 前は にぎやかです. Food is おいしい and weather is いい.
132前まえに
A: It's my first time in this canteen. B: ___ you eat, please wash your hands.
WHY Before an action is the dictionary form + 前に: 食べる 前に. あとで needs the た-form (食べた あとで), and ながら joins 食べ-.
143ながら
A: Do you listen to music at work? B: Yes, I always work ___ listening to music.
WHY Two things at once is the ます-stem + ながら: 聞きながら. たり, ても and てから need the て/た-form (聞いたり, 聞いても).
152に
A: Where are you going? B: I'm going to the post office ___ buy stamps.
WHY Going somewhere to do something is the ます-stem + に 行く: 買いに 行きます. を, で and へ cannot follow a verb stem.
164しまいました
A: What's wrong? You look down. B: I went and left my umbrella on the train.
WHY Regret about something that went wrong is 〜て しまいました. わすれて おきました would mean you left it there on purpose.
171とき
A: When you came to Japan, what was hard? B: Not understanding the language was hard.
WHY The time something happened is 〜た とき: 来た とき. から and ので give a reason and のに a contrast — none asks about that time.
182方かた
A: I don't know how to use this machine. B: Then let's try it together.
WHY How to do something is the ます-stem + 方: 使い方, how to use. 中, 前 and 後 do not join a verb stem this way.
192お疲つかれさまでした。
A: Well, I'll be going now. B: ___
WHY When a co-worker leaves work, say お疲れさまでした. おかえりなさい welcomes someone home; いってらっしゃい sees someone off from home.
203おじゃまします。
A: Welcome. Please come in. B: ___
WHY Going into someone else's home, say おじゃまします. ただいま is for your own home, and いってきます is said when you leave it.
212それは おめでとうございます。
A: I'm getting married next month. B: ___
WHY Good news like a wedding gets おめでとうございます. ざんねんですね is for bad news, and たいへんでしたね for a hard time.
221しつれいします。
A: Yes, come in, please. B: ___
WHY Going into an office, say しつれいします. おじゃましました is said when you leave someone's home, and いってきます when you leave your own.
232ごちそうさまでした。
A: You ate a lot! B: Yes, it was delicious. ___
WHY After a meal, say ごちそうさまでした. いただきます comes before eating, and おまたせしました means 'sorry to keep you waiting'.
242気きを つけて。
A: From tomorrow I'm going home to my country for a week. B: I see. ___
WHY To someone setting off on a trip, say 気を つけて, 'take care'. はじめまして is for first meetings; おかえりなさい welcomes someone back.
