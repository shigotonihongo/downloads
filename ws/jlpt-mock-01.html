こたえ · Answer Key

Each answer carries the reason it is correct — read the WHY even when you scored it right.

1C開あけて
It's hot. Please ___ the window.
WHY 暑あつい (hot) → you 開あける (open) the window. 閉しめる=close, 洗あらう=wash, 運はこぶ=carry.
2A起おきます
Every day I ___ at 6 in the morning.
WHY 朝あさ6時じ + 起おきる = get up. 寝ねる=sleep, 帰かえる=go home, 働はたらく=work — none of them fit a morning wake-up time.
3D安やすいです
'This bag is not expensive.' Which sentence is closest in meaning?
WHY 高たかい = expensive, so 高たかく ない (not expensive) = 安やすい (cheap). The other three describe age, size and schedule, not price.
4Cお疲つかれさま。
(At work) You: 'I've finished the work.' Senior: ___
WHY 「お疲つかれさま」is what you say to someone who has finished working. いただきます=before eating, はじめまして=first meeting, いってきます=leaving home.
5C郵便局ゆうびんきょく
You're going to buy stamps. Where do you go?
WHY 切手きって (stamps) are sold at the 郵便局ゆうびんきょく (post office). 銀行ぎんこう=bank, 図書館としょかん=library, 病院びょういん=hospital.
6B休やすんだ
You have a fever. You'd better ___ soon.
WHY With a 熱ねつ (fever) the advice is 休やすむ (rest): 休やすんだ ほうが いいです. 〜ほうが いい always takes the past plain form (た-form).
7C痛いたいです
I have a cold and a fever. My head also ___.
WHY あたまが 痛いたい = my head hurts. The other three describe fun, price and age — a body part takes 痛いたい.
8B祖母そぼ
My mother's mother is my ___.
WHY 母ははの 母はは = grandmother = 祖母そぼ. 祖父そふ=grandfather, 姉あね=older sister, 妹いもうと=younger sister.
9D図書館としょかん
You want to read a book. Where is a quiet place?
WHY 図書館としょかん (library) is the quiet place for 読よむ. 公園こうえん=park, 駅えき=station, 店みせ=shop — none of them are quiet.
10A牛乳ぎゅうにゅう
For breakfast I have bread and drink ___.
WHY 飲のむ needs a liquid: 牛乳ぎゅうにゅう (milk). 野菜やさい, 魚さかな and 卵たまご are all eaten (食たべる), not drunk.
11A明日あした
The day after today is ___.
WHY 今日きょう → the next day is 明日あした (tomorrow). 昨日きのう=yesterday, 今いま=now, 毎日まいにち=every day.
12D書かきます
I ___ a letter to my friend.
WHY 手紙てがみ takes 書かく (write). 飲のむ=drink, 着きる=wear, 話はなす=speak — none of them take a letter as an object.
13A150円えん
I bought three apples for 450 yen in total. Each one is ___.
WHY 450円えん ÷ 三みっつ = 150円えん each. ぜんぶで = in total, 一ひとつ = one item.
14C難むずかしい
This job is not simple. It's very ___.
WHY 簡単かんたんでは ありません (not simple) = 難むずかしい (difficult). The others describe enjoyment, price and size.
15Dいただきます。
(Before a meal) You say: ___
WHY いただきます is said BEFORE eating; ごちそうさま comes after. おやすみなさい=good night, ただいま=I'm home.
16D目め
The part you use to see is the ___.
WHY 見みる (to see) uses 目め (eyes). 耳みみ=ears (聞きく), 口くち=mouth (話はなす), 手て=hands.
17Dで
I go to the office ___ train.
WHY で marks the MEANS of transport: 電車でんしゃで 行いきます (go by train). へ already marks the destination (会社かいしゃへ), so に is not needed.
18Cと
Yesterday I went shopping ___ a friend.
WHY と = together with: 友ともだちと 買かい物ものを しました. を already marks the object (買かい物ものを), へ marks a direction.
19B着ついたら
When you arrive in Japan, please call me.
WHY 〜たら = when/after A happens, do B — and B CAN be a request (電話でんわして ください). With 〜と, B may not be a request, which is why 着つくと is wrong here.
20Bいけません
You must not smoke here.
WHY て-form + は いけません = prohibition (must not). 〜ても いいです = may; 〜なければ なりません = must do. 吸すっては ください is not Japanese.
21A日本語にほんごを
Put the four parts in order. Which one goes in ★?
WHY Correct order: 私わたしは 日本にほんで 働はたらきたいから 日本語にほんごを 勉強べんきょうして います。The reason (〜から) comes before the result, and 日本語にほんごを sits directly before its verb — so ★ is 日本語にほんごを.
22Bより
The bus is cheaper ___ the train.
WHY AはBより〜 = A is more ~ than B. 電車でんしゃより 安やすい = cheaper than the train. から=from/because, まで=until, でも=but.
23Bを
I eat ___ rice.
WHY を marks the direct object of 食たべます. が marks a subject, に a destination/time, で a place or means.
24Bへ
Next year I will go ___ Japan.
WHY へ marks the DESTINATION of 行いきます: 日本にほんへ 行いきます. より is for comparison, を for objects.
25Dいます
Right now I am studying Japanese.
WHY て-form + います = an action happening NOW. あります is for existence of things, not for a continuing action.
26Cこと
Have you ever been to Japan?
WHY 〜た ことが あります = have the EXPERIENCE of doing something. もの=thing (object), ところ=place, とき=time.
27Cなりません
Tomorrow you must come by 9.
WHY 〜なければ なりません = must / have to. It is a fixed pair: ければ always closes with なりません.
28Dしませんか
Shall we have a meal together this weekend?
WHY 〜ませんか is how you INVITE someone (won't you…?). しました is past, して います is ongoing — neither invites.
29Dいいです
Teacher, may I sit here?
WHY 〜ても いいですか asks PERMISSION (may I?). 〜ては いけません is the refusal, not the question.
30Dつもり
I plan to study at the library this weekend.
WHY plain form + つもりです = I intend/plan to. つもり always follows the DICTIONARY form (する), never します.
31A名札なふだ
Which item do you NOT need tomorrow?
WHY 制服せいふくは 要いります but 名札なふだは 要いりません — 要いりません = is not needed.
32B熱ねつが あるから
Why is Tanaka-san taking the day off?
WHY 熱ねつが あるので 休やすみます — ので gives the reason. The work is not cancelled, just moved to 明日あした.
33C10時じ
At what time can you go to the hospital?
WHY 受付うけつけ is 午前ごぜん 9時じ〜12時じ only; 午後ごごは 休やすみ. 10時じ is the one option inside it.
34A9時じ
Until what time is the supermarket open today?
WHY The usual close is 午後ごご8時じ, but 今日きょうは 9時じまで — today's notice overrides the normal hours.
35A食事しょくじ
What does the person who wrote the message want to do?
WHY 食事しょくじしませんか = won't you eat with me? 〜ませんか is an invitation, so 食事しょくじ is what they want to do.
36A2時間じかん
How long does the meeting take?
WHY 10時じから 12時じまで = two hours. 〜から〜まで gives the start and the end, and the gap is the length.
37Cスーパー
Which of these is nearby?
WHY 近ちかくに 駅えきと スーパーが あります — only the station and the supermarket are listed as nearby.
38A本ほんを 読よむ
Which of these is allowed in the library?
WHY Both rules are prohibitions: 話はなしては いけません and 飲のみ物ものは だめです. Reading a book is not forbidden.
39D手伝てつだい
What is this person asking for?
WHY 手伝てつだって くれますか = could you help me? 〜て くれますか is how you ask someone for a favour.
40B日本にほんで 働はたらきたいから
Why is this person studying Japanese?
WHY 日本にほんで 働はたらきたいです。ですから… — ですから introduces the RESULT, so the sentence before it is the reason.
