こたえ · Answer Key

Each answer carries the reason it is correct — read the WHY even when you scored it right.

12やさい
I eat a lot of vegetables every day.
WHY 野菜 is やさい (vegetables). 野 is read や here, not の, and さい does not change to ざい.
23ぎゅうにく
I bought 300 grams of beef at the supermarket.
WHY 牛肉 is ぎゅうにく (beef). ぎゅうにゅう is 牛乳, milk — same first kanji, different word.
31ようふく
I bought winter clothes at the department store.
WHY 洋服 is ようふく (clothes). ふく does not change to ぶく here, and よう keeps its long vowel: not よふく.
43夏
___ is hot, so I often swim in the sea.
WHY Hot weather and swimming in the sea → 夏, summer. 冬 is winter and 秋 is autumn.
52料理
On Sundays I do the ___ at home. I make curry.
WHY Making curry is 料理, cooking. 旅行 is a trip and 運動 is exercise — neither is done by making curry at home.
61図書館
I borrow books at the ___ and read them at home.
WHY You borrow books at a 図書館, a library. 美術館 is an art museum and 映画館 is a cinema.
73からい
This curry is very ___. I drank a lot of water.
WHY Hot, spicy food makes you drink water → からい. やすい (cheap) and つめたい (cold) give no reason to drink a lot of water.
81すきました
I'm ___. Shall we go and get something to eat?
WHY おなかが すきました means 'I'm hungry', so you invite someone to eat. おなかが いっぱい means you are full.
92きたない
My room is ___, so I'll clean it.
WHY You clean a room because it is きたない, dirty. あかるい (bright) and あたらしい (new) are no reason to clean.
103店みせの 前まえに 人ひとが ならんで います。
Which sentence uses ならぶ (to stand in a line) correctly?
WHY ならぶ: people stand in a line, 人が ならんで います. Waiting for a bus is まつ; putting plates out is ならべる.
111お祭まつりに 人ひとが おおぜい 来きました。
Which sentence uses おおぜい (many people) correctly?
WHY おおぜい is only for people: 人が おおぜい 来ました. For apples or water, use たくさん.
122秋あきの 朝あさは すずしいです。
Which sentence uses すずしい (pleasantly cool) correctly?
WHY すずしい describes air and weather: 秋の 朝は すずしい. A cold drink is つめたい; bath water that is not hot is ぬるい.
132飲のみたい
A: It's hot, isn't it? B: Yes, I'd ___ something cold.
WHY Wanting something is 〜たい, with が on the thing: つめたい ものが 飲みたいですね. 飲まない says the opposite.
143したり
A: What do you do at weekends? B: Things like reading and ___ cooking.
WHY Listing examples is 〜たり 〜たり します: 読んだり、料理を したり します. After 読んだり the second verb needs たり too, not して.
151おきましょう
A: I hear a typhoon is coming tomorrow. B: Then let's buy water today, ___.
WHY Getting ready in advance is 〜て おく: 買って おきましょう. 買って みましょう means 'let's try buying it' — not getting ready.
164帰かえる つもりです
A: What will you do in the summer holidays? B: I ___ go back to my country.
WHY Your own plan is 〜つもりです: 帰る つもりです. そうです and ようです report news or guesses, and 帰った ところ means you just got back.
174で
A: This Saturday there's a festival in front of the station. B: Oh, I'd like to go.
WHY An event 'happens' at a place with で: 駅の 前で お祭りが あります. に is for where a thing simply is: 駅の 前に 店が あります.
182くれた
A: That sweater looks nice. B: Thanks. My sister bought it ___ me.
WHY Someone does something for you: 姉が 買って くれた. もらった needs に (姉に 買って もらった), and あげた is for giving to others.
192いいえ、けっこうです。
A: Do you need a bag? B: ___ A: Then I'll hand it to you as it is.
WHY けっこうです politely says 'no, thank you', so the clerk gives it without a bag. いただきます is said before eating.
201こちらこそ、よろしく。
A: I'm Lin, I've just moved in next door. Nice to meet you. B: ___
WHY To よろしく お願いします you answer こちらこそ, 'likewise'. いってきます is for leaving home; しつれいします for entering or leaving a room.
213じゃあ、あとで かけるね。
A: Hello, can you talk now? B: Sorry, I'm on the train. A: ___
WHY Your friend cannot talk on the train, so offer to call later: あとで かけるね. いってらっしゃい sees someone off from home.
222では、いただきます。
A: I made plenty, so please help yourself. B: ___
WHY Before you start eating, say いただきます. ごちそうさまでした comes after the meal, and おじゃましました when you leave a home.
233すみません、わかりません。
A: Do you understand what this word means? B: ___ A: Then I'll explain it again.
WHY The teacher explains again, so the student did not understand: わかりません. よく できました is the teacher's praise, not an answer.
241ええ、おかげさまで。
A: Were you all right in yesterday's earthquake? B: ___
WHY When someone asks if you are all right, ええ、おかげさまで says 'yes, thank you'. おまたせしました is 'sorry to keep you waiting'.

女 みそは、火ひを 止とめてから 入いれて ください。
WHY 火を 止めてから means after turning off the heat. The vegetables go into the boiling water first; the miso comes last.
342黄色きいろい 箱はこ
Where do the bottle caps go?
女 ペットボトルは、キャップを とって、中なかを 水みずで 洗あらって ください。
女 ボトルは この 青あおい 箱はこ、キャップは となりの 黄色きいろい 箱はこに 入いれて ください。
WHY キャップは となりの 黄色い 箱 — the caps go in the yellow box next door. The blue box is for the bottles.
351夜よる 8時じ
From what time can people go into the school?
女 こちらは みどり市しです。今夜こんや、台風たいふうが 来きます。いちばん 強つよく なるのは 夜よる 11時じごろです。
女 みどり小学校しょうがっこうには 夜よる 8時じから 入はいれます。食たべ物ものは 学校がっこうに ありますから、水みずと タオルを 持もって 来きて ください。
WHY 小学校には 夜 8時から 入れます. 11時 is when the typhoon is strongest, not when the school opens.
363水みずと タオル
What should people bring?
女 こちらは みどり市しです。今夜こんや、台風たいふうが 来きます。いちばん 強つよく なるのは 夜よる 11時じごろです。
女 みどり小学校しょうがっこうには 夜よる 8時じから 入はいれます。食たべ物ものは 学校がっこうに ありますから、水みずと タオルを 持もって 来きて ください。
WHY Bring 水と タオル. Food is already at the school (食べ物は 学校に あります), so you do not need to bring it.
373佐藤さとうさんの 家いえ
Where is Lin's parcel now?
WHY Lin was out (留守), so Sato-san took it in for her (かわりに もらいました). It is at Sato-san's home, not at Lin's.
381夜よる 7時じから
When can Lin go to get the parcel?
WHY Sato-san is home from 7 p.m. (7時からは 家に います). 昼 is when the parcel arrived.
393金曜日きんようびの 夜よる
When is next week's class?
WHY Thursday's class is cancelled and held on Friday instead (その かわりに、金曜日). Wednesday is the e-mail deadline.
401水曜日すいようび
By when should students with questions send an e-mail?
WHY 水曜日までに メールして ください — by Wednesday. Thursday is the cancelled class; Friday is the new class day.
413魚さかなが おいしい
What does Maria say is good about Hokkaido?
WHY でも、魚が 安くて おいしい — 'but' marks the good point after the cold. Snow is part of the cold, and she never says winter is warm.
422冬休ふゆやすみ
When does Maria want Ann to visit?
WHY She is busy with work this month (今月は 忙しい), so she invites Ann for next month's winter holidays: 冬休みに 来ませんか.
4311,000円えん
You order the fish lunch and a coffee. How much is it?
WHY The fish lunch (B) is 900円 and coffee adds 100円: 1,000円. 950円 would be the hamburger lunch with coffee.
4422時じ
Until what time in the afternoon is lunch served?
WHY 14:00 is 2 p.m. (午後 2時). Don't read 14 as 4.
452四日よっかの 夜よる 8時じ
When are the fireworks?
WHY 花火は 四日の 夜 8時から — the 4th, Sunday, at 8 p.m. 午後 4時 is when the festival opens each day.
461次つぎの 週しゅうに なる
What happens if it rains?
WHY 雨の ときは、次の 週に します — it moves to the next week. It is not held in the rain, and it is not cancelled.
4722,000円えん
How much will 4,000-yen shoes cost in the sale?
WHY Shoes are 半分の ねだん, half price: 2,000円. 30% OFF is for shirts and trousers — that would be 2,800円.
4835,000円えん以上いじょう 買かった 人ひと
Who gets a free shopping bag?
WHY The bag goes to anyone who buys 5,000円 or more (5,000円以上). Buying certain items or coming early does not matter.
