家族かぞく

Family — Lesson 4

Can-do: Talk about your family members and their ages.

1 · Vocabulary — 語彙

#日本語RomajiEnglishメモ / Notes
1家族kazokufamily
2写真shashinphoto
3大きいookiibig
4父chichi(my) father
5母haha(my) mother
6兄ani(my) older brother
7何歳nansaihow old
8若いwakaiyoung

2 · Dialogue — 会話

Hana
これは 家族かぞくの 写真しゃしんです。
This is a photo of my family.
Aya
大おおきい 家族かぞくですね。
It's a big family, isn't it.
Hana
父ちちと 母ははと 兄あにが います。
I have a father, a mother, and an older brother.
Aya
お兄にいさんは 何歳なんさいですか。
How old is your older brother?
Hana
25歳さいです。
He is 25.
Aya
若わかいですね。
He's young, isn't he.
