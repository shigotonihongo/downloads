When a Resident Falls — Emergency Response — Scenario Dialogue

Can-do: Respond calmly when a resident falls: check them, tell them not to move, call for help, and reassure them.

1 · Dialogue & Why — 会話

Hana (caregiver)
山田やまださん、大丈夫だいじょうぶですか！
Yamada-san, are you all right?!
Why: The very first words at a fall: 大丈夫だいじょうぶですか checks consciousness and response. Say the resident's name to get their attention and check if they can answer you — that alone tells you a lot about their condition.
Yamada-san (resident)
いたい…。
It hurts…
Why: 痛いたい = 'it hurts'. In pain or shock, a resident will speak in short plain words. Your job is to stay calm and find out WHERE and HOW badly — next line.
Hana (caregiver)
動うごかないで ください。 今いま すぐ 人ひとを 呼よびます。
Please don't move. I'll call someone right now.
Why: 動うごかないでください uses 〜ないでください = 'please do NOT do ~'. After a fall you must keep the resident still — moving could worsen a fracture or injury. 今いますぐ = 'right now'; 人ひとを呼よびます = 'I'll call someone'. Never leave them and never move them yourself first.
Hana (caregiver)
だれか 来きて ください！ 山田やまださんが 転ころびました！
Someone come, please! Yamada-san has fallen!
Why: Call for help loudly and clearly: だれか来きてください = 'someone please come'. 転ころぶ = 'to fall over'; 転ころびました reports it in past polite. State WHO and WHAT so staff arrive ready. Reporting facts fast is the core of emergency 報告ほうこく.
Hana (caregiver)
どこが 痛いたいですか。
Where does it hurt?
Why: どこが痛いたいですか = 'where does it hurt?' — どこ (where) + が marks the painful spot. This tells you and the nurse where the injury is. Ask calmly; don't press or touch the area yet.
Yamada-san (resident)
足あしが…。
My leg…
Why: 足あし = 'leg/foot'. The resident names the painful part. Note it exactly to report to the nurse — 足あしが痛いたい ('the leg hurts').
Hana (caregiver)
わかりました。 そのまま 待まって いて ください。 すぐ 看護師かんごしさんが 来きます。
Understood. Please stay just as you are. The nurse is coming right away.
Why: そのまま = 'as is / without changing position'; 待まっていてください = 'please keep waiting'. Together: 'stay exactly as you are' — reinforcing don't-move. Then reassure with concrete help: すぐ看護師かんごしさんが来きます ('the nurse is coming right away').
Yamada-san (resident)
こわいです。
I'm scared.
Why: こわい = 'scared/afraid'. A fall is frightening for an elderly person. Acknowledging their fear and staying beside them matters as much as the physical care — next line.
Hana (caregiver)
大丈夫だいじょうぶですよ。 心配しんぱいしないで。 そばに いますから、安心あんしんして ください。
It's all right. Don't worry. I'm right here, so please rest easy.
Why: そばに = 'right beside'; いますから = 'because I am (here)' — から gives the reason to feel safe. 安心あんしんしてください = 'please be at ease'. Presence + reassurance calms the resident and prevents them from moving out of panic. 心配しんぱいしないで (心配しんぱい from Ep 11) is the plain, close-in form — right for someone frightened on the floor.
